① Mix: each hybrid = s + p

Amplitudes ψ, drawn to scale: in every direction the hybrid's amplitude is the s amplitude plus the p amplitude, signs included, so s builds up the + lobe and eats into the − lobe. The 3D view shows |ψ|². Dashed circle = the whole 2s. The numbers are coefficients; their squares are the shares (0.58² = 1/3).

Each row is one hybrid; the squares in a row add to 1. Any rotation of a relaxed set is equally valid (textbooks put h1 along x).
