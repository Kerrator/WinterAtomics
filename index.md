# About

<p class="intro-highlight">I am a PhD candidate in computational materials science at Queen's University, supervised by Laurent Karim Béland in the Department of Mechanical and Materials Engineering. I use density-functional theory and molecular dynamics to study how defects form, segregate, and move in engineering alloys.</p>

<div class="bio-section">
<h2>Research</h2>

My PhD work examines how chromium changes the vacancy concentration and the segregation of aluminum to grain boundaries in nickel-based alloys. Both affect oxidation resistance at high temperature. This work is published in <em>Scripta Materialia</em> (<a href="https://doi.org/10.1016/j.scriptamat.2026.117203" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">2026</a>). A companion page with short walkthrough videos from my talk on PyKMC (Ni–Cr–Al kinetic Monte Carlo) is at <a href="/pykmc/">winteratomics.ca/pykmc</a>. Earlier research explored ion-pair trapping in functionalized organic systems for energy storage and light detection. See <a href="/research">Research</a> for details.

</div>

<div class="bio-section">
<h2>Methods</h2>

I run density-functional theory calculations in Quantum ESPRESSO and molecular dynamics in LAMMPS, and I analyze the results in Python. The full list of tools is on the <a href="/research#methods--tools">Research</a> page.

</div>

<div class="bio-section">
<h2>Teaching</h2>

I teach undergraduate courses in materials science and chemistry at Queen's University, and I coordinated laboratory sections for first- and second-year engineering and science courses at Ontario Tech. My teaching emphasizes reproducible computation, hands-on problem-solving, and responsible use of AI tools in scientific work. See <a href="/teaching">Teaching</a> for course details.

</div>

<div class="bio-section">
<h2>Background</h2>

MSc in Materials Science (2023) and BSc in Chemistry (2021) from Ontario Tech University. Outside research and teaching, I experiment with local language models and generative tools to develop safer, verifiable workflows for learning and scientific communication.

</div>
